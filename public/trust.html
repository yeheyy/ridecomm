🔐 Trust Certificate

Required once per iPhone for microphone access

⚠️ This certificate is only for your local hotspot. It never leaves your private network and cannot access the internet.
⬇️ Step 1 — Download Certificate
✅ Certificate downloaded! Now follow the steps below.
2

Install the Profile

After downloading, go to:
Settings app → at the very top you'll see
Profile Downloaded → tap it
→ tap Install → enter your passcode → tap Install again.

3

Trust the Certificate

Go to:
Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
→ Under CERTIFICATE TRUST SETTINGS
tap RideComm Local CA
→ toggle Enable Full Trust → tap Continue

4

Allow Microphone

When you open the app and tap JOIN, Safari will ask for microphone access.
Tap Allow — this only happens once.

5

Open the App

Tap the button below. The connection error will be gone and mic will work! 🎙️

🏍️ Open RideComm App
